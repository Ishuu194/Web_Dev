India is a beautiful and diverse country in South Asia. Its capital is New Delhi, and the Indian Rupee (₹) is its currency. India is famous for its rich culture, traditions, festivals, food, historical monuments, and natural beauty. The Taj Mahal, Red Fort, India Gate, and Gateway of India are some famous places in India. The national animal of India is the Bengal Tiger, and the national bird is the Peacock. India celebrates Independence Day on 15 August and Republic Day on 26 January. India is known for its unity in diversity, with many different languages, religions, cultures, and traditions.
