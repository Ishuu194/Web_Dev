Hii August

August is the eighth month of the year with 31 days. It is named after Emperor Augustus Caesar and is known for its important historical events, cultural festivals, and seasonal changes. In India, August is especially significant because of Independence Day and many traditional festivals, making it a month of patriotism, joy, and celebration.

August is a month of harvest, festivals, and celebrations in many countries. In India, several festivals often fall in August, such as: Raksha Bandhan Krishna Janmashtami Nag Panchami Independence Day (15 August)
